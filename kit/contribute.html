Contribute a laptop record

Help build a price estimator for second-hand laptops on the LUCT Market. Fill one record per laptop you know about. Nothing is uploaded automatically — you download a CSV and hand it to the project team.

Why we ask. We are building a tool that estimates a fair asking price for second-hand laptops. Your contributions make that estimate realistic for Lesotho instead of relying on foreign data.

Voluntary and anonymous. This is entirely optional and you can skip any field. We collect laptop information only — never your name, email, phone number, student number or any other personal detail. Data is used only to estimate prices and is reported in aggregate.
